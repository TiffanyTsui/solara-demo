Platform · methodology

One data layer · three modelling chains · two regulatory frameworks.

SOLARA's modules sit on a common inventory: whatever a module computes becomes a flow in the life-cycle ledger and a row in the CSRD/ESRS disclosure tables.

Life-cycle scope · PEFCR for Fresh & Frozen Vegetables

SOLARA follows the European Commission's Product Environmental Footprint Category Rules (PEFCR) for fresh and frozen vegetables. Eight cradle-to-grave stages; solid borders are stages where SOLARA captures primary data, dashed borders use PEF default scenarios for downstream consumer behaviour.
EF 3.1 PEFCR v1.3 ISO 14040 / 14044 CSRD-ESRS-aligned

Modelling chain — solar & energy module

Three physics engines chained at hourly resolution. Each module owns one engine; outputs hand off as well-defined data products.
