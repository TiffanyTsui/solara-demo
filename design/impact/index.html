Environmental Impact (PEF)

A single Product Environmental Footprint that travels from feasibility through CSRD/ESRS disclosure. The Module-04 engine pulls bill-of-materials, energy, and water flows directly from Modules 01–03 — no separate LCA project file required.

Life-cycle scope (PEFCR for Fresh & Frozen Vegetables)

Cradle-to-grave, eight stages. The red dashed boundary marks the system boundary; "Use" and "End-of-life" are scoped but use defaults from PEF Annex II because consumer behaviour dominates.

Why one module for LCA and CSRD

Most companies build two parallel data stacks — one for the LCA practitioner, one for the CSRD/ESRS report. SOLARA collapses them. A single inventory of every flow that crosses the system boundary is sufficient to populate both the PEF result tables and the ESRS disclosure tables; the engine just re-aggregates by indicator family. That means one source of truth, one set of audit evidence, and zero double-entry.

A draft PEFCR-aligned LCA for the Farm32 Morocco vs. Kenya scenarios is already complete (see the methodology page); Module 04 productises that workflow into the same UI as the other design modules.
