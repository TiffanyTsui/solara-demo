How the week numbers work
Weeks follow the ISO-8601 calendar.
A year has 52 or 53 weeks — 2026 has 53, so the plan runs to W53. A batch is planted on its week's Monday and harvested in the week its growing days end; batches planted late in the year harvest early in the next year, so labels always carry the year (e.g. 2027-W02). A section counts as occupied until its harvest week, when it is cleared and replanted.
