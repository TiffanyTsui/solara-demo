Design · Agadir (indoor · new build)

Pre-build design chain

Site selection, facility energy, energy management, and environmental impact, all on one physics model. The flow below shows how outputs hand off downstream.

Module flow — click a step to open

Site & climate sets the boundary conditions. Facility energy converts geometry & production schedule into an 8760-hour load. Energy management dispatches PV and battery against that load. Environmental impact carries the bill of materials, energy, and water into PEF-aligned indicators. Every module also feeds Operate once the facility is live.
