Validation

SOLARA's energy model reproduces Graamans (2018) at ≈ 235 kWh per kg lettuce dry weight versus the published 247 kWh / kg DW — within 5%. For Farm32's basil schedule (42,250 kg fresh weight per year) the specific energy is 16.5 kWh / kg-FW, below the LCA-reported 26.4 kWh / kg-FW because dehumidification overhead and capital-goods stages sit outside the EnergyPlus boundary. Those gaps close in Module 04 (Environmental Impact).
