Why intake order matters
Realized prices per color are the highest-value stream.
The plan currently optimizes against the estimated price curve. Three to five years of realized weekly prices per color turn the Women's-Day scenarios in Plan from assumptions into fitted distributions — and unlock per-color planting decisions across all five locations.
