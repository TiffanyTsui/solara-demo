Product 2 · Pre-season

Cultivation planning — what to grow, when, and how much

SOLARA Plan turns a crop ledger — cycle lengths, yields, prices, labor, and the energy costs coming out of SOLARA Design — into a profit-maximal planting schedule, solved as a linear / mixed-integer program in milliseconds. Every scenario is a data override; every constraint reports its shadow price. The engine is facility-agnostic: indoor farm or greenhouse is a property of the ledger, not the model.

Product 1 · Design

Decide what to build

Site, geometry, equipment, PV + storage sizing. Outputs the energy cost profile per season that Plan prices cultivation against.

Product 2 · Plan

Decide what to grow

Ledger-driven optimization of the planting calendar under space, labor, and rate constraints — with scenario stress-tests before the season commits.

Product 3 · Operate

Verify and re-plan

The plan becomes the baseline the live twin tracks. Actual yields, cycle times, and prices flow back into the ledger for the next re-solve.

Case 2 · Greenhouse — same engine, different ledger

Six-crop cut-flower greenhouse, 10,000 m²

13-period LP · validated vs published thesis

To show the engine generalizes beyond indoor farms, the same ledger-driven builder reproduces a published cultivation-planning model: Kromhout (2007), a Dutch cut-flower greenhouse with six crops, thirteen 4-week periods, and ground + labor constraints. Five of six published experiments reproduce to the euro; the sixth differs −0.03% due to rounding in the thesis' hand-edited coefficients.

Base plan — planting by crop and period

m² planted per 4-week period. The LP fills all 10,000 m² year-round while the labor roster allows.

Shadow prices — what another m² is worth

Dual value of the ground constraint per period (EUR/m²). Where it is high, expansion or faster turnover pays; where zero, ground is not the bottleneck.

Scenario experiments — every one a data override

No model edits: each experiment overrides ledger rows (labor limits, crop set, energy surcharge, labor minutes, contract floors) and re-solves in milliseconds.

Methodology

One ledger, one solver, any facility

Model

Decision variables are planted area (or beds) per crop and period/week. Constraints: space occupancy over the cycle, labor per period, planting-rate caps, contract floors. Objective: net margin at harvest-time prices minus inputs, energy, and labor.

Facility-agnostic by data

Indoor vs greenhouse is a ledger property: a greenhouse ledger stretches the winter cycle (light-limited); an indoor ledger keeps the cycle constant and moves the seasonal energy price instead — priced from SOLARA Design's PV + battery dispatch.

Solver

HiGHS via SciPy — LP for area plans, MILP for integer bed/bay decisions. The 2007 six-crop problem and the 52-week Farm32 problem each solve in milliseconds, so rolling re-solves every week are trivial.

Learning loop

SOLARA Operate feeds actual cycle times, yields, and realized prices back into the ledger. The plan is never a one-off study: it is re-solved on a rolling horizon as the season reveals itself.

From plan to live baseline

The plan is what Operate holds you to
